# masomode

## About

Maso Mode is a Minecraft mod that makes the game more challenging in a vanilla friendly way

Mobs are tougher, starvation is a real threat, revamped progression, additional status effects and events to punish you further

There are new advancements to guide you trough the game

The mod is inspired by Fear & Hunger, Terraria and BTW

## Changes and additions

### Herbs

Herbs are important for survival since they can be used to treat wounds and cure infection and other status effects

Herbs can spawn naturally in certain biomes, it's also dropped sometimes by witches and it can be found as random chest loot

#### Blue herb

* Found in the plains and forest biomes
* Restores health

#### Green herb

* Found in the desert and jungle biomes
* Cures infection

#### Red herb

* Found in dark forest and swamp biomes
* Restores small amount of health
* Can be combined with blue or green herbs to enhance their effect

### Status effects

#### Bleeding

Getting hit by an arrow has a chance to cause bleeding

Bleeding is similar to poison, but it causes less damage, however it doesn't go away with time

Use a paper to treat bleeding

#### Infection

Zombies cause infection

Infection does nothing however once the timer runs out it kills you

Use a green herb to treat infections

#### Fear

Fear is caused by being in the dark

Fear makes your hunger drain faster

If you are in the dark for too long it can also invert your keybinds and it will eventually kill you

#### Paralyzed

Drowned and cave spider can paralyze you if you get hit by them

If you are paralyzed you can't move and you can't perform certain actions

#### Bone fracture

Bone fracture is caused by getting hit with an axe or by falling from a great height

Bone fracture decreases your maximum HP and it doesn't go away with time

Drink milk to treat bone fracture

### Gameplay mechanics

* Certain animals will fight back if you attack them, animals are also targeted by mobs
* No ambient light, caves are pitch dark, without a light source you get the fear status effect
* Campfires and torches are not lit by default, use a fire starter to light them
* Pillaring up is not possible anymore
* Entities can no longer be trapped with boats
* Boats no longer cancel fall damage
* More blocks are affected by gravity
* New moons are replaced with blood moons
* Burning arrows can ignite blocks around them
* Burning entities can ignite blocks around them
* Campfires can ignite blocks around them
* Mobs have an increased follow range
* All villages are replaced with zombie villages
* Nerfed random chest loot
* Nerfed axe damage
* Nerfed shields, every attack will disable it for a certain time, getting hit with an axe breaks them
* Being on fire for too long will kill you
* Drinking a water bottle will extinguish fire

### Crafting

Multiple recipes have been changed and some got removed

Certain recipes are only accessible if you are close enough to specific workstations

Fletching table

* Arrows, bows and crossbows

Loom

* Recipes with wool

Grindstone

* Iron weapons and tools

Smithing table

* Iron armor and other recipes with iron

Cauldron

* Mixed herbs, stews and soups

Anvil

* Recipes with diamond

Enchanting table

* Eye of ender and golden fruits

### Food mechanics

* Natural regeneration is heavily nerfed from food and saturation, use herbs to treat your wounds
* Actions that cause exhaustion drain your hunger even more
* All raw foods can cause the hunger status effect
* Sugar is now edible

### Mobs

#### Creeper

Creepers lunge at you and explode slightly faster, they can also explode trough walls

#### Zombie

Getting hit by a zombie or a zombie variant will cause infection

Drowned can paralyze you if you get hit by them

#### Skeleton

Skeletons no longer burn during the day, getting hit by a skeleton can cause bleeding

#### Spider

Spiders are smaller, and they can cause poison for a few seconds if you get hit by them

Cave spiders can paralyze you

#### Enderman

Being close to an enderman will cause blindness for a few seconds and fear

If an enderman is angry at you, you will constantly get the fear status effect until you kill it

If an enderman can't reach you during the fight, it will teleport you away to a place it can reach you

#### Blaze

Getting hit by a blaze speeds up the on fire timer to kill you

#### Ghast

Ghasts attack faster and they require a smaller height difference than before to attack you

### Blood moon

New moons are replaced with blood moons

During blood moon

* Zombies have a higher chance to call for reinforcements if you hit them
* Skeletons shoot flaming arrows and there is a chance for them to shoot multiple arrows, their arrows sometimes get replaced with fireworks
* Creepers have a chance to get charged, charged creepers don't have to get as close as normal creepers to explode
* Spiders spawn with the invisibility status effect
* Illusioners can spawn
